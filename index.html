关于本站

本站由中国 FRC 社区共同维护，面向新老队伍，汇集官方资源、零件采购、 编程与机械设计指南、赛事时间线和注册流程，全站中英双语。

FRC Wiki CN is a community-maintained guide built by and for Chinese FIRST Robotics Competition teams. It brings together what a team actually needs — official resources, parts suppliers, programming and mechanical design guides, event timelines, and registration steps — with bilingual (中文 / English) explanations throughout.
