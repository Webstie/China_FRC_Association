欢迎 Welcome

Whether you're starting a brand new FRC team or you're a veteran looking for resources tailored to the Chinese robotics community, this wiki gathers everything you need in one place — from official information sites and parts suppliers, to programming tutorials, mechanical design guides, event schedules, and step-by-step registration walkthroughs.

无论你是刚刚组建新队伍，还是想为中国FRC团队寻找更适合的资源， 这里汇集了你所需要的一切：官方信息网站、零件采购渠道、编程教程、 机械设计指南、赛事日历，以及详细的注册流程。
