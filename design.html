Drivetrain Options 传动系统

选择合适的传动方案是赛季初最重要的设计决定之一。

  • Tank Drive 坦克底盘 Simplest, cheapest, most reliable. Great for first-year teams. 最简单可靠，适合新队伍。
  • West Coast Drive (WCD) Tank drive with dropped center wheel — improves turning. 经典进阶方案。
  • Swerve Drive 全向轮 Each wheel independently steers. Maximum maneuverability but complex and expensive. Lorem ipsum.
  • Mecanum / H-Drive Less common in modern FRC. 现代 FRC 较少使用。

Design Principles 设计原则

  1. KISS — Keep It Simple, Stupid. 简单方案往往更可靠。
  2. Iterate fast — Build a prototype before finalizing CAD. 先做原型再画图。
  3. Design for manufacture — Avoid features your team can't actually make.
  4. Bumper rules — Always design around the bumper-zone constraint.
  5. Weight budget — 125 lb limit. Track weight in your CAD assembly.
