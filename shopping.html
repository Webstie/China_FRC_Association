Shopping & Suppliers

购物与供应商 — 零件采购指南

International Vendors 国际供应商

These are the major US-based FRC suppliers. They ship internationally but expect long lead times and customs duties.

  • AndyMark Drivetrain kits, gears, wheels, structural components. See the AndyMark purchase process for China teams.
  • REV Robotics NEO motors, SPARK MAX, MAXTube, ION build system. See the REV purchase process for China teams.
  • West Coast Products (WCP) Swerve modules, gearboxes, drivetrain components. See the WCP purchase process for China teams.
  • VEX Pro VersaPlanetary gearboxes, structural parts, sensors. See the VEX Pro purchase process for China teams.
  • CTR Electronics Kraken X60 motors, Pigeon 2 IMU, CANcoders. See the CTRE purchase process for China teams.

Chinese Suppliers 国内供应商

对于中国队伍，以下渠道可以买到许多兼容零件，并且发货快、运费低。

  • 淘宝 Taobao 铝型材、轴承、链条、皮带、3D打印耗材等通用零件。
  • 京东 JD.com 电子元件、工具、电源等 — 通常发货更快、有正品保障。
  • 阿里巴巴 1688 批发渠道，适合大宗采购原材料。
