Registration Guide

注册指南 — 新队伍报名流程

Overview 概述

Registering a new FRC team involves several steps: creating accounts on the FIRST Inspires Dashboard, paying registration fees, signing up for specific events, and submitting required documents. The process is the same worldwide, but Chinese teams should plan extra time for payment processing and visa logistics.

中国队伍报名 FRC 需要在 FIRST Inspires 官网完成多个步骤。 因为涉及国际汇款和签证，建议尽早开始。

Step-by-Step 详细步骤

Step 1 — Create a Lead Mentor account 创建主导师账户

Go to firstinspires.org and create a new account. The lead mentor (主导师) must be an adult and will manage the team's registration and roster.

Step 2 — Start a new team 创建新队伍

In the dashboard, select "Start a Team" and choose FRC. You'll be asked for school/organization information, team location, and contact details. After submission, FIRST assigns a team number — this is your permanent identity in the FRC community.

Step 3 — Pay registration fees 缴纳注册费用

The base team registration fee is approximately $6,000 USD for new teams (includes a Kit of Parts and one event entry). Additional events cost ~$5,000 USD each. Fees can be paid via credit card or wire transfer.

💰 中国队伍注意 国际汇款可能需要 1–2 周到账，请预留充足时间。部分队伍通过香港或海外赞助方代付， 以避免外汇额度限制。

Step 4 — Choose events 选择赛事

Once registration is paid, select your competition events from the event list. Popular events fill up quickly — register on the day event signup opens (usually early October).

Step 5 — Complete Youth Protection 完成青年保护培训

All adult mentors must complete the YPP (Youth Protection Program) screening before the team can be considered "rookie cleared." This includes a background check and online training.

Step 6 — Build your roster 录入队员名单

Add all student team members and mentors to the dashboard roster. Every student must have a signed consent form on file before competing.

Required Documents 必备文件

  • Consent & Release forms 同意书 One per student team member. Available in the dashboard.
  • Lead Mentor agreement 主导师协议 Signed by the lead mentor of record.
  • School/sponsor authorization 学校或赞助方授权 如果队伍挂靠学校或机构，需要对方出具授权或支持证明。
  • Visa / travel documents 签证材料 For events outside China — passports, invitation letters, etc.

Funding 资金筹集

Registration + travel + parts often totals $20,000–$50,000+ USD per season. Common funding sources:

  • School / education foundation funding 学校资助
  • Corporate sponsorship 企业赞助 — 本地企业或跨国公司赞助
  • NASA / FIRST grants (rookie teams only) 新队伍补助
  • Parent contributions and crowdfunding 家长与众筹

FAQ 常见问题

Q: 我们没有学校支持，可以参加吗？

Yes — community teams (non-school-based) are welcome. You'll need a registered organization (非营利或企业实体) as the sponsoring entity.

Q: 报名截止日期？

Registration technically stays open through the season, but events fill up. Aim to complete registration by mid-October；每年具体日期以官方公布为准。

Q: 还有其他问题？

Email frcteams@firstinspires.org or post in Chief Delphi. Many veteran Chinese teams are happy to help new teams — see the Info page for community resources.
